# Names, looks and licences

Riffhouse stays free and open, and it stays out of trouble. These rules apply to the app, the tones, the website and anything posted about it.

## Gear

- Every amp, pedal and cabinet is an original design. Never use the name, logo, badge, colour scheme or overall look of a real product.
- Names describe the sound or the type: "Green Drive", "Big Fuzz", "Funk Filter". No brand or model names and no near copies of them or of their logo lettering (for example no Cry Baby, Big Muff, Tube Screamer, RAT, Klon, Hot Rod, Twin, AC30, JCM, 5150, Rectifier).
- Describing a sound in plain words is fine: "the classic 60s wah sweep", "British crunch".

## Tones named after players

- Use "Inspired by <Artist>: <Song>". It describes a style, nothing more.
- No photos, logos, album art, signatures or lyrics of any artist, in the app or in posts.
- Never use an artist's name, face or song to advertise Riffhouse: no artist names in ads, reel captions, thumbnails or sponsored posts. Saying the app has tones for many styles is fine.
- If an artist, band or their team asks for a tone to be renamed or removed, do it in the next release, no argument. Ask through an issue on GitHub.

## Other software

- Riffhouse is AGPL-3.0-or-later. Anything built into it must be compatible: MIT, BSD, Apache 2.0, MPL 2.0, LGPL, GPL 3.0 and the SIL Open Font License are fine. Nothing "non-commercial only", no "free for personal use" fonts, no code copied from closed products.
- Every part built in gets its licence text added to `cmake/Notices.cmake`, so it lands in `Licences.txt` beside the app.
- Amp captures (.nam files) and cabinet impulses are not shipped unless they are our own or carry a licence that allows sharing. Players load their own.
- ASIO and VST are trademarks of Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH.

## Pictures and sound

- Screenshots, renders and sounds of Riffhouse are ours. Stock photos only with a licence that allows it, credited where the licence asks.
- Demo clips and reels use our own playing or music we have the rights to. Covers of songs are posted only where the platform licenses music (Instagram and YouTube do for most songs).
