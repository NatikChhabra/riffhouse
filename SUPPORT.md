# Getting help with Riffhouse

Riffhouse is free and made by a small team, so here is the quickest way to a fix.

## 1. No sound, crackles, delay, a driver that will not open
Read [docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md). It covers most setups, including Bluetooth delay and Exclusive Mode.

## 2. Using Riffhouse inside a DAW
See [docs/DAW.md](docs/DAW.md).

## 3. Something is broken
In the app, press **Send a report**. It sends the app's log privately, with your Windows user name and PC name removed, and it tells us the version and settings we need. This is the fastest route.

No report button because the app will not start? [Open an issue](https://github.com/NatikChhabra/riffhouse/issues/new/choose) and say:
- your Riffhouse version (Settings, About, or the installer file name)
- your Windows version
- your audio interface or sound card, and whether you use ASIO, WASAPI or the Windows driver
- what you did, what you expected and what happened

## 4. Questions, tones and chat
Join the [Riff House Discord](https://discord.gg/my4Mgg8uTq).

## 5. Security problems
Please do not post these in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What to expect
We read every report and issue. Fixes ship as updates, and the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) lists what changed. Riffhouse is free for players, always, and support is a best effort run by people, not a paid help desk.

## Privacy
What can leave your PC, and when, is listed in [docs/PRIVACY.md](docs/PRIVACY.md).
