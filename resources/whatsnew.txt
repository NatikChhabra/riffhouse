# What the in-app "What's new" panel shows. Written by hand for players, newest version first.
# Keep each version to 3-5 short lines per section: the full list lives in CHANGELOG.md.
# The heading is the version; a release cannot be tagged without its entry (tools/release/notes.py check).

## 0.1.7
### New
- A Reduce knob on the Gate quiets the hiss between notes and under their tails, so distortion tones are much quieter between notes.
- A feedback killer runs with the gate: it notches a howl that only ever grows, and never touches played notes.
- The Pitch pedal's Shift mode is rebuilt and sounds much cleaner on notes and chords.
- Settings has an update channel (Stable or Beta) and a Community section with the Discord server, YouTube, Instagram and the website.
- Tones are named for the sound they make, not for people, bands or songs. Mark a favourite again if its name changed.
- The weekly player count is off until you say yes once.

## 0.1.6
### New
- The metal amps now play captures of real amps, so palm mutes stop dead and the drive cleans up when you play softer.
- A noise gate that sets itself: Auto is the default on every tone.
- Three new cabinets recorded from real 4x12s, with a mic knob that moves through real mic positions.
- A Feedback button next to the name: say what sounds wrong and it goes to the people who fix Riffhouse.
### Fixed
- Clean tones sounded dull. Glass Clean and Chime Top are brighter on top, like the amps they are modelled on.
- The gate no longer chatters on USB guitar cables that tick.
- Setup no longer keeps a mode that hears your guitar quieter.
- A broken or half-downloaded capture file could crash Riffhouse. It now says the file is damaged.
- The Lo-fi pedal's Downsample knob glides with no click.
