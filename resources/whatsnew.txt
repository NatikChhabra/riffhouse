## 0.1.6
### New
- The metal amps now play captures of real amps, so palm mutes stop dead and the drive cleans up when you play softer.
- A noise gate that sets itself: Auto is the default on every tone.
- Three new cabinets recorded from real 4x12s, with a mic knob that moves through real mic positions.
- A Feedback button next to the name: say what sounds wrong and it goes to the people who fix Riffhouse.
- This panel, and Community links in Settings with the Discord server.
### Fixed
- Clean tones sounded dull. Glass Clean and Chime Top are brighter on top, like the amps they are modelled on.
- The gate no longer chatters on USB guitar cables that tick.
- Setup no longer keeps a mode that hears your guitar quieter.
- A broken or half-downloaded capture file could crash Riffhouse. It now says the file is damaged.
- The Lo-fi pedal's Downsample knob glides with no click.
