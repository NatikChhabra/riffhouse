The facts

What
A guitar amp and pedalboard. A standalone app and a VST3 plugin.
Price
Free. No paid version, no account, no ads.
Runs on
Windows 10 and 11.
Inside
11 amps, 30 pedals, 9 cabinets, 189 tones. Tuner, looper, chords and tabs, play along.

A guitar and a cable. A laptop’s mic jack is enough.

Ready words

Three lengths. Paste them as they are or cut them to fit.

  • One line

    Riffhouse is a free, open-source guitar amp and pedalboard for Windows.

  • Short

    Riffhouse is a free guitar amp and pedalboard for Windows, as a standalone app and a VST3 plugin. It has 11 amps, 30 pedals and 189 tones, and it works through a laptop’s mic jack with no audio interface.

  • Long

    Riffhouse is a free, open-source guitar amp and pedalboard for Windows. Plug a guitar into an audio interface or straight into a laptop’s mic jack, strum for twenty seconds while it sets itself up, and play. Inside are 11 amps, 30 pedals, 9 speaker cabinets and 189 ready tones, with a tuner, a looper, chords and tabs, and a recorder that lets you change the tone after you have played. There is no paid version, no account and no ads. It is built by one person, Natik Chhabra, and every release stays free. Music should be free.

  • The name. Riffhouse, one word.
