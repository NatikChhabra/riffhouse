  1. 0.1.6

    Out now 17 November 2026

    Update 1: real amp captures

    • The four high-gain amps now play captures of real amps.
    • Four new 4x12 cabinets, made from recordings of real ones.
    • A noise gate that sets itself, saved with each tone.
    • Brighter clean amps.
    • Setup hears USB guitar cables right.
    • A Feedback button at the top.

    Full notes for 0.1.6

  2. 0.1.5

    13 November 2026

    Pedals worked out as real circuits

    • Every drive and fuzz, solved as its circuit.
    • Hum and steady whines, learnt and cut out.
    • Eight Indian rock and indie tones. 197 in all.
    • No clicks on any switch or knob.
    • Send a report in one click.

    Three hotfixes, the last on 14 November (0.1.5.3). Held notes ring out, two sound cards play steady, and you can type a knob’s value.

    Full notes for 0.1.5 Notes for Hotfix 3

  3. 0.1.4

    12 November 2026

    Finds the fast way in by itself

    • A slow start retries the quickest audio mode by itself.
    • Settings is one page.
    • Setup listens to guitar inputs first, webcams last.

    Full notes for 0.1.4

  4. 0.1.3

    12 November 2026

    Chords, tabs and a classic wah

    • Song sheets: chords over the words, and tabs you play from.
    • Throat, a classic sixties wah.
    • Share a tone as a plain message.
    • A new logo, and a window that fits any screen.

    Full notes for 0.1.3

  5. 0.1.2

    12 November 2026

    It stays open

    • 0.1.1 crashed on real PCs. This fixed it by the next morning.
    • No driver to pick: Riffhouse finds one that works.
    • Every build now runs a real setup and a real start before it ships.

    Full notes for 0.1.2

  6. 0.1.1

    11 November 2026

    The first public release

    • 11 amps, 9 cabinets and a full pedalboard.
    • Setup that finds your guitar and sets the level by itself.
    • Record a take, then change its tone afterwards.

    Full notes for 0.1.1
