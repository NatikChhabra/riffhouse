Out now 0.1.4

Next 0.1.5

The polish release. Sound you can trust.

  • Real circuit pedals

    Every drive and fuzz, solved as its circuit.

  • Cleaner high gain

    Less digital fizz on high notes.

  • Quiet when you stop

    The gate shuts with your hand, and hum goes.

  • A gentler treble fix

    Mic jacks get less brightening and less hiss.

  • Indian rock and indie tones

    A new pack.

  • A weekly player count

    Anonymous. One switch turns it off.

  • Fixes from your reports

    Press Save a report in Settings. Every report gets read.
