Riffhouse is a free guitar amp and pedalboard for Windows. Plug into the mic jack, strum for twenty seconds, and play.

A computer’s mic jack eats a guitar’s treble. Riffhouse measures the loss while you strum and puts it back.

  • Auto level on

  1. It finds your guitar

    Strum, and it picks the input your guitar is on.

  2. It picks the lowest delay

    It keeps the fastest audio mode that runs without crackles.

  3. It sets your level

    Play your hardest for four seconds.

  4. It measures the room

    The gate sits above your noise, and mains hum is removed.

Hear it

One guitar part, recorded once through a mic jack, played back through different tones.

    Record first, tone later

    Every take is saved twice: once the way you heard it, once as the clean guitar. Pick another tone later and your own playing runs through it.

    Press L for your takes. Change the tone while a take loops, turn the knobs, and save the result as a new take.

    Load a song. Slow it to 50 percent and the pitch stays. Move the key and the speed stays. Mark the hard part and it goes round until you have it.

    Press O to record a part, O again to let it go round, and once more to add a layer. Solo over your own rhythm.

    Standard, drop, open or tuned down: it shows the nearest note and how far off you are. No tuning to choose first.

    What we measure

    Every change runs 5,900 automatic checks on the sound engine before it ships.

    • 5 samples

      Delay added by the rig. About 0.1 ms.

    • 8x

      Amp oversampling.

    • 42.9 dB

      Aliasing on a hot amp.

    • 1/8 of a core

      Everything switched on.

    • About 5 dB

      Loudness spread across the tones. With Auto level on.

    Questions

    Anything else, ask on GitHub.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No price, no account, no ads in the app and no paid tier, now or later. The code is open under the AGPL-3.0 licence.

    Do I need an audio interface?

    No. A cable from your guitar to the computer’s mic jack is enough. If you own an interface, pick it as your input. Its maker’s ASIO driver is used when it works.

    Will I hear myself late?

    The rig adds about 0.1 ms. The rest is your computer’s audio buffer, and setup picks the shortest one that runs clean.

    Can I use my own captures?

    Yes. Any .nam file loads as the amp and any impulse response as the cabinet. Drop the file on the window.

    Are the artist tones official?

    No. “Inspired by” names a style. Riffhouse has no tie to any artist, band or equipment maker.

    Is there a Mac or Linux version?

    No installer yet. You can build it from source today.

    Something is broken. Where do I say so?

    Start with Help and fixes. Still stuck? In Settings, press Report a problem. It copies a report and opens the GitHub page to paste it into. Tone requests go there too.
