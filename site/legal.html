Privacy policy

Riffhouse has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything that can leave your PC is listed here.

The weekly player count

On the first run Riffhouse asks whether to count you. If you say yes, once a week it sends the week (like 2026-W47), a random number made on your PC (not from your name, your PC or your sound card) and the app version. It goes to a private sheet only the maker can open, with the time it arrived. Your internet address is not stored. It tells us how many people play each week. You can change your answer any time: Settings, Player count. Copies built from source send nothing.

Updates

Riffhouse asks GitHub for the newest version, the same as opening the download page. Settings controls whether it installs updates by itself. GitHub counts installer downloads, with nothing about who.

Problem reports

Only when you press Send a report. It sends the app log and settings with your Windows user name, PC name and any email addresses taken out first. At most three a day.

This website

The site counts visits and clicks on its buttons with a small script of our own. For a visit it records only the page and the site you came from (like reddit.com); for a click, which button. Counts are added up per hour and only totals are kept. Your internet address is not stored.

Update emails (only if you sign up)

If you leave your email on the updates page, it goes into a private list with the date and a random code for leaving. You get one email when an update ships, nothing else. It is never shared or sold. Every email has a link that removes you straight away.

The uninstall question

If you answer “Why did you uninstall?”, it sends the reason you picked, the short note you typed (up to 300 letters) and the app version. Nothing that says who you are. Please keep personal details out of the note.

Your rights

The random number is the only thing that could ever point back to one install. To have it deleted, or an email address removed, or to ask what is held, write to the contact below with the first lines of your random number (Settings shows it) or the email you used. We reply within 7 days. Players of any age can use Riffhouse: nothing is collected beyond the items above, and there is nothing to sign up for.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no tracking IDs. The only thing it may keep in your browser is a note that you chose not to be counted, if you ever use that option. No banner is needed because nothing is tracked.
