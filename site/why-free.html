Why it’s free

I wanted a good guitar tone. Then I looked at the price.

Everything costs money

  • Amps cost money.

  • Pedals cost money.

  • Even the app that pretends to be an amp costs money.

So I built my own

It’s free. Fully free. No free trial.

Download for Windows See all the gear

It’s not a luxury

Music should be free. It’s open to everybody.

So a guitar and one cable is enough. Plug into the mic jack your computer already has, and Riffhouse puts back the treble the jack takes away. Nothing to buy first.

So who pays

  1. Today

    Nobody. I build it alone. If it helped you play, you can chip in.

  2. Later

    Companies, not players. A brand pays for a tone pack, and you get it free.

  3. Never

    A paid version, locked tones, ads in the app or an account.

I’m just one guy

I’m trying my best, okay? I’ll show you every step of the way, including the part that breaks my soul.
